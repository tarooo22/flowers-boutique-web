# Homepage override

Use this concise order:

1. Light 45/55 split hero with one real bouquet image
2. Live category links
3. Approximately eight live products
4. One simple Bouquet Builder benefit section
5. Three-step ordering/delivery information
6. One short real-photography brand moment
7. Configured contact actions
8. Minimal light footer

Homepage-specific rules:

- No dark overlay, carousel, floating cards, sticky story, parallax or oversized editorial gaps.
- Use translation keys for all new customer-facing content.
- Categories link only to real catalog category IDs.
- The hero is the only image with eager loading and high fetch priority.
- Product loading, error, empty, unavailable and missing-image states remain intentional.
- Do not explain Visual versus AI modes on the homepage; sell one clear customer benefit.
- Do not show testimonials, ratings, delivery guarantees or other unsupported claims.
