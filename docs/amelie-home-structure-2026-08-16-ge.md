## Structural constraints

Reference header is not a secondary mobile sheet masquerading as navigation. It is a compact public commerce shell with a small top announcement line, a visible nav hierarchy and separated utility controls. Product modules are image-led, borderless or near-borderless white surfaces with an upper-right favourite affordance, a short copy stack and price below the media.

The corrective Home must therefore remove the current Flowers Boutique experience-first composition, large rounded dark builder panels, delivery-step section and contact-card ending as the primary page rhythm. Existing data queries, links, cart callback, language copy and SEO are reusable; their previous presentation order is not.

## Clean-room boundary

Amelie branding, image content, product names/prices, cashback policy, marketing claims and private source assets must not be copied. Flower’s Boutique retains its own wordmark, images, product inventory, data and routes inside an independently implemented version of the public observable layout.
